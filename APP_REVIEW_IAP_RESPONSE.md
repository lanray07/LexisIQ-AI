# App Review Response - In-App Purchases

Paste this into the App Review reply:

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the review. The in-app purchases are available inside the app using these steps:

1. Launch LexisIQ AI.
2. Complete onboarding by leaving the default selections and tapping Generate Legal Workspace.
3. On the LexisIQ Command Desk, tap the Chambers intelligence available banner, or open the More tab.
4. In More, tap Plans & Subscription.
5. The subscription options shown on that screen are:
   - Professional Monthly: lexisiq.professional.monthly
   - Professional Yearly: lexisiq.professional.yearly
   - Chambers Monthly: lexisiq.chambers.monthly
6. Tap Upgrade on any paid plan to open the Apple StoreKit purchase sheet. Restore Purchases is available on the same screen.

No login or special account is required. The in-app purchases are not restricted by storefront, account type, or device configuration.

Before resubmitting, confirm this in App Store Connect:

- The Paid Apps Agreement is active.
- The subscription group and all subscription products have complete metadata, pricing, localization, and review screenshots.
- The products above are attached to the same review submission as iOS App 1.0. The submission should include the app version plus each subscription product being reviewed.
- If only one subscription should be reviewed, remove the unavailable products from the app UI before resubmitting.
